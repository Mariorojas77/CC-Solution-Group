CNC Machining Capabilities

Machined plastic components built around your drawing.

CNC plastic machining provides a repeatable way to produce custom shapes and features from plastic sheet and plate. The machining approach depends on the material, thickness, geometry, dimensions, quantity and finish requirements of the project.

Send CC Solution Group your drawing or CAD file along with the known project requirements so the part can be reviewed as a complete custom plastic fabrication project.

Plastic Materials

CNC machining for acrylic and engineered plastics.

Material selection should be based on the application and required performance. Depending on the project, custom plastic fabrication may involve materials such as:

  • Acrylic / PMMA
  • Polycarbonate
  • HDPE and UHMW
  • PVC
  • PETG
  • ABS

Drawing / CAD

Material & Thickness

Specify the plastic material and thickness when already determined.

Quantity & Requirements

Include quantity, dimensions, tolerances and desired delivery date when known.
