Plastic Bonding & Acrylic Assembly for Custom Fabricated Products.

CC Solution Group supports bonded acrylic and plastic components, multi-piece assemblies and fabricated subassemblies built around customer drawings and project requirements. Bonding and assembly can be combined with machining, cutting and forming to create finished custom plastic products.

Acrylic Bonding

Joining fabricated components into complete assemblies.

Many custom plastic products require separate pieces to be fabricated and joined into a finished assembly. Acrylic bonding can be used for clear fabricated products, covers, enclosures, display components and other multi-piece designs.

The fabrication approach depends on the material, geometry, joint configuration, appearance requirements and intended use of the finished product.
