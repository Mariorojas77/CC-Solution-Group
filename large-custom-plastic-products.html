Multiple Fabrication Processes

Machining, forming, bonding and assembly working together.

Large custom plastic products often depend on several fabrication processes. Individual sections may be CNC machined or cut, formed into shape, bonded and then assembled into the finished product.

  • CNC plastic machining and routing
  • Custom cutting and panel fabrication
  • Plastic bending and forming
  • Acrylic and plastic bonding
  • Multi-piece assembly
  • Custom covers, guards and enclosures
