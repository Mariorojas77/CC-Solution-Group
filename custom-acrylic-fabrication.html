Acrylic Fabrication

Clear plastic fabrication built around the application.

Acrylic is used in many fabricated products where appearance, clarity and custom geometry are important. A project may require machining, cutting, forming, bonding or a combination of fabrication processes.

We review the part geometry, material thickness, quantity and assembly requirements to help define the fabrication approach for the project.

Machining + Bonding + Forming

A complete custom fabrication approach.

Many acrylic products require more than one fabrication process. Machined pieces may need to be formed, bonded or assembled into a finished product. Keeping the project centered on the final application helps determine which processes make sense together.
